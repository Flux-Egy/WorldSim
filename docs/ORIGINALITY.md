# Originality and sourcing policy

> "It's fine to quote and learn from others, but not to steal from them, because nobody finishes alone."
> (owner, 2026-11-19)

WorldSim's art, audio, UI and code are **our own work**, so the project holds the rights to everything it ships.

## Allowed: learning and inspiration
- **Genre conventions.** World sandboxes, god-view simulation, pixel art, tile maps, eras and kingdoms are shared ideas that nobody owns.
- **Studying open-source projects** to learn techniques: architecture, algorithms, shaders, pixel-art methods. We then write our own version.
- **Using code from open-source projects** only when its licence allows it (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, zlib, CC0 and similar). Each use is listed in `CREDITS.md` with its licence, and the licence text is kept in the repo.
- **Tutorials, articles, talks and books** on pixel art, colour theory, animation, procedural generation and game design.
- **Reference photos** and real-world observation, such as how trees sway or how water moves.
- **Free asset packs (for example CC0) as study material.** We can look at how a good 16×16 tree is shaded, then draw our own. Packs are never shipped in the game.
- **Ideas and suggestions** from the owner, friends, players and AI tools. Any art generated by AI is reviewed and reworked to fit our palette and style.

## Not allowed: taking
- Anything extracted from a commercial or proprietary game: decompiled or ripped code, sprites, audio, prefabs, data tables or documents derived from them. This holds even if the files are renamed, edited, recoloured, re-uploaded somewhere, or claimed to be "found on GitHub".
- Tracing, recolouring or lightly editing someone else's sprites and calling them ours.
- Code or art whose licence does not allow it, or whose licence is unknown.
- False credits, meaning a source line that hides where something really came from.

## How our art is made
1. **Procedural C#** (`Assets/WorldSim/Runtime/Render`) draws terrain, water, plants, creatures and icons from code, and is tested in `Tools/CoreTests`.
2. **Hand-made or tool-made PNGs** are created for WorldSim (by the owner, by Claude through the repo's art tools, or AI-assisted and then reworked). They follow `docs/ASSET_SPEC.md` and are loaded with `PngDecoder`.
3. Every non-trivial borrowed technique or code snippet gets a line in `CREDITS.md`.

When in doubt, we don't use it, and we ask.
