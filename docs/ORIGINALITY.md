# Originality and sourcing policy

Policy updated by the owner on 2026-11-21: "We're not here to prove we make everything from scratch."
It replaces the 2026-11-19 policy, which allowed no ready-made packs at all.

## Allowed
- **Style, ideas and mechanics from any game, WorldBox included.** Genre conventions, the look and feel, and how systems play are free to learn from and imitate.
- **Ready-made assets, code and audio whose licence allows it.** For example CC0, CC-BY, MIT, OGA-BY, BSD, Apache-2.0 or zlib, from sources such as Kenney, OpenGameArt or free packs on itch.io.
- **Our own art pack (`Assets/WorldSim/Resources/WorldSimArt`) stays the base.** External packs complete it or improve it.
- **Reference images and videos of other games (WorldBox included), for the look only.** They stay **outside the repo** (for example in `.local-reference/`, which is gitignored).

## Conditions for every external asset, snippet or sound
1. **Check the licence before downloading or using it.** Keep the licence text in the repo next to the files (for example `Assets/ThirdParty/<pack>/LICENSE.txt`).
2. **Add a row to `CREDITS.md`** with the name, author, source, link, licence and where it is used in WorldSim.
3. **The credits are shown in the game** (Settings > Credits, generated from `CREDITS.md`).
4. **Follow the licence terms** (attribution, share-alike, and so on).
5. **Never claim it as ours.**

## Not allowed
- Ripping or decompiling any commercial game (WorldBox included): code, sprites, audio, data tables, or documents derived from them. This holds even if the files are renamed, edited or "found online".
- Paid assets that weren't bought.
- Anything with an unknown licence, or a licence that doesn't allow our use.
- False or missing credits. **A credit is not a permission:** crediting something whose licence forbids our use does not make it allowed.

When in doubt, don't use it, and ask the owner.
